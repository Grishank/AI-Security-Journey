# 🧠 Room Overview

This room introduces **AI Forensics (AI DFIR)** and explores how Artificial Intelligence enhances digital forensic investigations. It covers the role of AI in evidence analysis, incident response, and the legal and ethical considerations involved in AI-assisted investigations.
