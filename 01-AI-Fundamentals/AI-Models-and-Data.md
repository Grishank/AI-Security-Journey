# 🧠 Room Overview

This room explains how AI models are built, the importance of high-quality training data, and the security challenges associated with AI systems. It also introduces concepts such as inherited bias and the black-box nature of modern AI models.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand the role of training data
- Learn how AI models are developed
- Explore inherited bias in AI systems
- Understand the black-box problem
- Learn why data quality is critical for AI security

---

# 📌 Room Summary

### Task 1 — Introduction
- Overview of AI models and the importance of data in modern AI systems.

### Task 2 — Training Data
- Learned how AI models rely on large, high-quality datasets and how poor or malicious data can affect model performance.

### Task 3 — Building the Model
- Explored the basic AI model training process, from data collection to model deployment.

### Task 4 — The Inheritance Problem
- Learned how AI models inherit bias, errors, and vulnerabilities from their training data.

### Task 5 — The Black Box Problem
- Explored the challenges of understanding how complex AI models make decisions.

### Task 6 — Practical
- Reinforced the concepts through practical exercises and knowledge checks.

# 🛡️ AI Security Relevance

AI security begins with trusted data and secure model development. Understanding how models learn, inherit bias, and make decisions is essential for identifying AI vulnerabilities, preventing data poisoning, and building secure, reliable AI systems.

---

# 💬 Key Takeaway

> "The security and reliability of an AI model depend not only on its algorithms but also on the quality, integrity, and transparency of the data used to build it."
