# 🧠 Room Overview

This room introduces the core concepts of **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, **Machine Learning (ML)**, **Neural Networks**, **Deep Learning**, and **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. It provides the foundation needed before exploring AI security threats and defenses.
