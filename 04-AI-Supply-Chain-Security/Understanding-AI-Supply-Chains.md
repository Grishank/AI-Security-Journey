# 🧠 Room Overview

This room introduces the **AI supply chain** and explains how AI systems rely on external models, datasets, frameworks, packages, and APIs. It highlights how each dependency can become an attack vector and why securing the entire AI ecosystem is essential.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand AI supply chains
- Learn how AI systems consume external resources
- Identify supply chain attack layers
- Explore common AI supply chain risks
- Study real-world AI supply chain incidents

# 🛡️ AI Security Relevance

AI systems inherit risks from every external dependency they rely on. Securing the AI supply chain helps reduce the chances of compromised models, poisoned datasets, and vulnerable software components.

---

# 💬 Key Takeaway

> "An AI system is only as secure as the models, data, libraries, and infrastructure it depends on."
