# 🧠 Room Overview

This room explores how trusted AI components such as **models, repositories, dependencies, APIs, and prompt templates** can become attack vectors. It demonstrates common supply chain attacks and highlights techniques to secure AI systems against compromised dependencies.

# 🛡️ AI Security Relevance

Modern AI applications rely heavily on third-party components. Securing models, dependencies, APIs, and repositories is essential to prevent supply chain compromises that could affect entire AI systems.
