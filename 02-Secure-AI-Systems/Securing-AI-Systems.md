# 🧠 Room Overview

This room introduces the architecture of modern AI systems and explains how to identify security risks across the AI lifecycle. It explores AI attack surfaces, system-level threats, secure design principles, and practical auditing techniques for building trustworthy AI applications.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand the architecture of AI systems
- Identify AI attack surfaces
- Learn common system-level threats
- Explore secure design patterns
- Practice auditing AI systems

---

# 📌 Room Summary

### Task 1 — Introduction
- Overview of AI system security and the importance of securing every component in the AI pipeline.

### Task 2 — Anatomy of an AI System
- Explored the major components of an AI system, including data sources, models, APIs, infrastructure, and users.

### Task 3 — The AI Attack Surface
- Learned how attackers can target different layers of an AI system, including data, models, APIs, and supporting infrastructure.

### Task 4 — System-Level Threats
- Studied common threats affecting AI systems such as data poisoning, prompt injection, model theft, and unauthorized access.

### Task 5 — Secure Design Patterns
- Explored security best practices including least privilege, trust boundaries, input validation, monitoring, and defense-in-depth.

### Task 6 — Auditing TryAssist: A Conversation with the System
- Applied security concepts by reviewing an AI application to identify risks and recommend security improvements.

# 🛡️ AI Security Relevance

Securing AI requires protecting every stage of the AI lifecycle—from data collection and model training to deployment and user interaction. Understanding AI architectures and attack surfaces enables defenders to design resilient, secure, and trustworthy AI systems.
