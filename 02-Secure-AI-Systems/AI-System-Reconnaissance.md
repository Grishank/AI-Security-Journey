# 🧠 Room Overview

This room introduces **AI System Reconnaissance**, focusing on how attackers and defenders identify AI infrastructure, fingerprint machine learning services, enumerate exposed AI components, and map the attack surface of AI-powered applications.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand AI infrastructure components
- Learn AI service fingerprinting techniques
- Enumerate exposed AI systems
- Map AI attack surfaces
- Explore AI reconnaissance detection methods

- Explored the architecture of AI systems, including models, APIs, frameworks, cloud services, and supporting infrastructure.

### Task 3 — Fingerprinting AI Services

- Learned a structured approach for AI reconnaissance and explored defensive techniques to detect and monitor reconnaissance activity.

# 🛡️ AI Security Relevance

Reconnaissance is the first phase of many cyber attacks. Understanding how AI infrastructure can be discovered and profiled enables security teams to reduce exposed assets, strengthen defenses, and detect malicious reconnaissance before exploitation occurs.
