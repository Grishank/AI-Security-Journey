# 🧠 Room Overview

This room introduces **AI Threat Modelling**, teaching how to identify, assess, and mitigate security risks throughout the AI system lifecycle. It explores AI-specific attack surfaces, adapts traditional threat modeling frameworks for AI, and applies industry standards such as **MITRE ATLAS** and the **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications**.

---

# 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Understand AI-specific attack surfaces
- Learn AI threat modeling methodologies
- Adapt STRIDE for AI systems
- Explore the MITRE ATLAS framework
- Map AI risks using the OWASP LLM Top 10

# 📌 Room Summary

### Task 1 — Introduction
- Overview of AI Threat Modelling and its role in designing secure AI applications.

### Task 2 — AI-Specific Assets and Attack Surfaces
- Identified AI assets such as models, datasets, embeddings, prompts, APIs, and inference pipelines, along with their potential attack surfaces.

### Task 3 — Data Supply Chain and STRIDE's Gaps
- Explored why traditional STRIDE does not fully address AI-specific risks and how AI introduces new security challenges.

### Task 4 — Adapting STRIDE for AI Systems
- Learned how traditional threat modeling techniques can be extended to identify AI-focused threats across the AI lifecycle.

### Task 5 — MITRE ATLAS: The AI Threat Technique Catalogue
- Explored MITRE ATLAS and its role in documenting adversarial AI attack techniques and defensive strategies.

### Task 6 — OWASP LLM Top 10: Mapping Risks to Components
- Learned how the OWASP LLM Top 10 helps identify and mitigate risks affecting Large Language Model applications.

### Task 7 — Practical: Threat Modelling MegaCorp's AI Assistant
- Applied AI threat modeling techniques to identify security risks and recommend mitigations for an AI-powered assistant.
